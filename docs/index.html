Five stages

Each stage explains itself. A failed intel source does not stop the rest, and a high-risk action stays in dry-run until an analyst approves a known playbook.

01

Detect

Brute force, spray, impossible travel, MFA fatigue, and a quiet path for a normal login.

02

Enrich

AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal, GreyNoise, OTX, Tor, GeoIP, ASN, and a local blocklist.

03

Score

Threat, behavior, impact, and correlation, with confidence scored separately. Cap is 90.

04

Respond

Allowlists, rate limits, approval, and pre-approved playbooks. Dry-run stays on.

05

Report

Text and HTML, with ATT&CK mapping and an explicit list of what could not be verified.
